Combat Stats of TTT

Stats in TTT

Power

Base unit of physical and magical power. Used to calculate all forms of damage. For physical attacks, contributes directly to ability effect outcome calculations. Magical attacks convert a portion of this number into magical power, that is then used in the final calculation. Physical attacks balance against magical attacks by using a higher fraction of this number for direct damage application. Most physical attacks, being close range, do not afford characters the benefit of distance from potential counterattackers. Physical attacks also do not typically carry many lingering effects, or complex status afflictions. Due to this, magical classes will need to rely more on Intellect (a secondary stat) to scale damage dealt. Dexterity

Base unit of dextrousness of a character. This stat is used to determine a character's ability to perform dextrous activities like lockpicking, dodging (if implemented), parrying and recovery/avoidance of certain status effects (eg. bound, pin/grip).

Speed

Base unit for determining the distance a character is capable of moving per turn. This stat is sometimes modified by dexterity to calculate dodge chance. Physical classes will tend to have more of this than magical classes; in particular rogue-like classes will be able to move quickly. Some abilities allow characters to mitigate the negative effects of low speed (eg. teleporting, blinking, lunging etc)

Intellect

Base unit of mental power a character is capable of. This stat is used in combination with power to calculate magical power for attacks and effects. This stat is also used in combination with energy to determine max limits on the character's ability resource.

Health

Base unit of health for a character. This determines how many max hitpoints a character has. Can be used with other stats to determine health-related stat modifications.

Energy

Base unit of capability for characters. This is typically a measure of max ability resource units (eg. mana, piety, rage) a character can have. Characters with high energy are capable of undertaking many energy-costly attacks.

Defense

Base unit of protection for a character. This number determines the mitigation factor for incoming damage to a character. Physical damage is straightforwardly calculated, but magical damage is mitigated using a slightly different set of factors. Defense can be temporarily reduced, bypassed, and augmented in combat.

Magical Defense

Modified defense stat by characters with defense modification. Intellect indicates a character's mental fortitude. With this stat, characters can be braced against most forms of magical damage. This stat will always be a fraction of the character's total defense, with a large portion of the character's physical defense usually being converted to this stat.

Focus

Base stat determining the concentration of the character. This stat controls the likelihood of landing critical hits, and the likelihood of recovering from some status effects (eg. sleep, stun).
