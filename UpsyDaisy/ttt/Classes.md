## Aquamancer
Magic-based class that uses water to damage and frustrate enemies. Uses offensive water magic to damage enemies and sullies the ground with puddles and ice to confound enemy mobility.
- Moderate damage

- High crowd control

- Average mobility
## Pyromancer
Highly offensive magic-based class that uses fire to damage and punish enemies. Uses offensive fire magic to damage and burn enemies. Ember placement is used to punish enemies for free-movement, as are fissures. Physically frail.
- High damage
- Moderate passive damage
- Average mobility
